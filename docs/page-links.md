## Study abroad guide sources

- [European Commission: studying abroad](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/studying-abroad)
  — study mobility, duration, Learning Agreements, recognition, and funding.
- [Erasmus+ Programme Guide: higher education mobility](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/programme-guide/part-b/key-action-1/higher-education-mobility-projects)
  — blended intensive programs, physical and virtual components, and ECTS credits.
- [IAU Short Term Study Abroad](https://www.aydin.edu.tr/en-us/international/shortterm/outgoing/Pages/Short-Term-Study-Abroad.aspx)
  — partner program types and possible fee discounts or scholarships.

The guide explains the formats; it does not announce an open Erasmus+ call,
guarantee grant funding, or confirm credit recognition for an individual course.

Outgoing Students is a submenu button, not a separate page. Click its label or
arrow to expand/collapse the four outgoing-program and guidance links.
