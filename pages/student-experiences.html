Experiences

What is it like to study abroad for a few weeks? Explore life in and beyond the classroom, what students bring home, and what they wish they had known before leaving.

Stories from abroad

These sample stories summarise first-hand accounts published by other universities. This collection will be replaced with stories from IAU students once the office has their written permission.

No stories match this combination. Choose another country or programme type.

Summer school Netherlands

A week tackling the energy transition

University of Groningen · Energy Challenge

Management and engineering · 2023

A master’s student worked through an energy simulation and visited a hydrogen research centre. Discussions with a small international group showed how law, economics and engineering shape the same energy decisions. The group kept debating the topic over dinner.

Practical tip

Compare the organiser’s accommodation with nearby options and leave room for informal time with classmates.

Language programme Spain

The Spanish learned over dinner

University of Granada · Spanish Language Immersion

Morning classes gave a visiting student phrases to try at dinner with a host family. Conversations were difficult at first, but repeated practice with a flatmate and the host helped the student speak more confidently. Flamenco workshops and trips added a cultural dimension.

Practical tip

If you stay with a host family, try using one expression from class at the dinner table each day.

Winter school Japan

Japanese lessons and a first tea ceremony

Kindai University · Winter School Japan

Mechanical engineering · 2024

An engineering student spent three weeks learning Japanese in Osaka. Local students joined the lessons, and afternoons included martial arts, a tea ceremony and a trip to Kyoto. The experience helped the student feel capable of travelling independently afterwards.

Practical tip

Learn to recognise hiragana and katakana before departure if you are new to Japanese.

Summer school Denmark

A familiar subject from a different perspective

Aarhus University · AU Summer University

Criminology and psychology · 2018

A student took a social science elective on youth, drugs and alcohol in Aarhus. The course showed how cultural attitudes can shape responses to the same issue. Staying within walking distance of campus also made it easy to meet other summer students.

Practical tip

Look for a short elective that complements your degree and ask where participants usually stay.

Winter school South Korea

A friendship that began at the airport

Hanyang University · Hanyang Winter School

Commerce · 2024

A commerce student struck up a conversation during a shared airport pickup. Classes and orientation brought more opportunities to meet people, and the new group later explored cafés and joined a ski trip. Making friends came more naturally than the student had expected.

Practical tip

Use small openings such as shared transport and orientation to start conversations.

Summer school United Kingdom

From the lab bench to the hospital bedside

University of Birmingham · Biomedicine summer school

Biomedicine

A biomedicine student spent three weeks combining laboratory work with time exploring Birmingham. The lab sessions provided experience the student expected to use at home, while getting to know classmates from several countries gave everyday practice speaking English.

Practical tip

Check how much time the timetable sets aside for hands-on work and group activities.

Language programme Germany

Finding the courage to speak German

Akademie für internationale Bildung · Summer in Bonn

2023

A student placed in an advanced German class found that understanding the language came more easily than speaking it. Conversations with a host helped the student relax and participate more in class. By the end, speaking felt comfortable enough for independent travel.

Practical tip

Give yourself time to settle into the level, and build low-pressure conversation practice into the day.

Winter school South Korea

Making winter break count toward a degree

Yonsei University · Winter at Yonsei

Economics minor · 2025

A senior used a winter session to fit study abroad around the degree. The econometrics course was relevant to the student’s minor, but getting the home department to recognise the credit took time. Advance planning helped make the short visit manageable.

Practical tip

Ask your own faculty about credit recognition early and keep a copy of the host’s syllabus.

Summer school South Korea

Learning to get around Seoul independently

Korea University · Summer study abroad

Psychology

A psychology student went to Seoul for six weeks and initially struggled with restaurants and public transport. Learning everyday Korean phrases made getting around easier. A visit to Gyeongbokgung Palace also introduced history the student had rarely encountered in classes at home.

Practical tip

Practise basic phrases before you leave and ask the programme team how to contact them if you need help.

Language programme France

Building speaking confidence in Nice

Faculty-led French language and culture programme

Accounting and French · 2011

A student spent six weeks living with a host family and taking French classes in Nice. Class trips offered a closer look at local history, while finding the right bus or ordering food meant using French outside the classroom. The student returned more willing to speak up in class.

Practical tip

Make a habit of handling one everyday task in the language you are learning.

Winter school Germany

Arriving alone and finding a group

University of Tübingen · International and European Studies Winter School

A student travelled alone to study a subject outside their major. A buddy group and an early castle outing eased the nerves. Back in class, discussion about how Germany remembers the Holocaust highlighted perspectives that were new to the student.

Practical tip

Join the first buddy activity if one is offered. Meeting a few people early can make the rest of the programme feel less unfamiliar.

Summer school Germany

Seminar discussions and a city to explore

Humboldt University of Berlin · Global Cities seminar

Educational science

A master’s student described seminar discussions shaped by a group with varied backgrounds. Outside class, a Spree boat trip and visits to German government buildings gave the student different ways to learn about Berlin alongside classmates.

Practical tip

Check which excursions are included and allow time for the ones that connect with your interests.

Language programme China

Learning Mandarin and planning for arrival

Tongji University · Summer School

Chinese language · 2024

A student found that conversation-heavy morning classes made everyday Mandarin easier to follow. Local student volunteers helped the group understand campus life. The first day proved harder because the group landed without mobile internet and had trouble arranging transport and paying for essentials.

Practical tip

Save arrival directions offline and arrange mobile access and a backup payment method before departure.

Summer school United Kingdom

Taking history beyond the classroom

University of Glasgow · History of Christianity in Scotland

History and German studies

A history student alternated class days with free days and visited the Isle of Iona on a weekend trip. Conversations in Glasgow felt easier than the student had expected, and free summer festivals offered an accessible way to spend time in the city.

Practical tip

Look up free local events and check whether excursions connect to the topics you will study.

Winter school South Korea

Public health through classmates’ perspectives

Hanyang University · Hanyang Winter School

A student interested in public health valued the cross-cultural discussions in class. Student ambassadors helped the visiting group get around Seoul and ran optional evening activities. The short format also made the trip possible around family responsibilities.

Practical tip

Ask how long you would be away and what arrival and on-campus support is available.

Language programme Germany

Making room for German in an engineering degree

TU Braunschweig · Summer school

Environmental engineering · 2024

An environmental engineering student spent two months in Braunschweig pursuing an interest in German. The student credited time with classmates from different countries, trips in the region and using the language day to day with making the summer valuable.

Practical tip

If your degree schedule is crowded, check whether a summer course and home-university funding can make language study possible.

Winter school Germany

Reading Berlin through its neighbourhoods

Humboldt University of Berlin · Winter University

Commerce

A commerce student took an urban development course that paired lectures with neighbourhood visits and also explored historical museums. Speaking up in small English-language classes built confidence. The intensive schedule also made the student rethink how to organise study time.

Practical tip

Set aside time to review your notes during an intensive course, especially if it includes frequent field visits.

Language programme Costa Rica

Catching the rhythm of everyday Spanish

USAC · Summer programme in Heredia

Spanish and entrepreneurship · 2023

A student arrived in Costa Rica on a first overseas trip and found everyday Spanish hard to follow at first. Living with a host family provided a quieter place to settle in, while a group hike gave another way to connect with classmates. By the end, the student could follow and take part in conversations with less hesitation.

Practical tip

Ask speakers to repeat themselves when needed. Notice how much easier familiar conversations become over time.

Summer school United Kingdom

Learning British heritage beyond campus

University of Birmingham · British Culture and Heritage

English language and literature

A student combined campus lectures with guided visits to places such as Oxford, Cambridge and Liverpool. Staff and student ambassadors helped the group navigate the busy schedule. The mix of classroom teaching and travel made the subject easier to connect with places and people.

Practical tip

Review the timetable and credit information, and ask which visits are included before budgeting.

Winter school Germany

Trying a university before applying for graduate study

University of Tübingen · International and European Studies Winter School

A student used the winter school to see whether Tübingen might be a good place for graduate study. Courses on European politics and economics offered a sense of the academic environment. Exploring the region, practising German and getting to know the buddy network helped the student picture daily life there.

Practical tip

If you are considering further study, use a short programme to ask current students what studying and living there is like.

Share your experience

Have you participated in a short-term programme? Share what you learned with the Short-Term Study Abroad Office and help the next student prepare.

Email the office ↗ Office contact details
What to include
  • Your first name or initials, faculty or department, and graduation year if you want it shared.
  • The programme name, host university, country and year. Say whether it was a summer school, winter school or language programme.
  • In 150–250 words, describe your academic, cultural and social experience. Include one thing you wish you knew before going and what you took away.
  • Optional: one or two photos you have written permission to share.

The office needs written permission before publishing your story, name, quotes or photos.
