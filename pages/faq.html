01

Programme basics

Understand your options.

What is a short-term study abroad programme?

A focused learning experience abroad, such as a summer or winter school, an educational camp, or a language course. The subject, length, and learning activities depend on the host programme.

Explore short-term study abroad →

What is the difference between a summer/winter programme and a language programme?

Summer and winter schools often centre on an academic subject or practical project. Language programmes focus on developing language skills. Both may include cultural activities, so compare the course content rather than the season alone.

See Summer & Winter Programmes → See Language Programmes →

Is every programme an Erasmus+ programme?

No. A partner university’s short course is not automatically an Erasmus+ activity. Erasmus+ mobility has its own institutional arrangements and selection process. Confirm the route with IAU before assuming that Erasmus+ funding or recognition rules apply.

Read about Erasmus+ and short-term options →
02

Applying

Know your next step.

How do I know whether I am eligible?

Read the eligibility section in the programme listing, then check the host’s current announcement. Requirements vary. Ask the listed contact if anything is unclear before submitting an application.

Check Summer & Winter Programmes → Check Language Programmes →

Do I apply through IAU or directly to the host university?

Follow the route given in the programme announcement. Some opportunities use a host application; others require IAU involvement. For Erasmus+, contact the IAU Erasmus+ Office about its selection process. If the route is unclear, ask before applying.

Find the programme’s application instructions → Ask the IAU office →

What happens if I miss a deadline?

Contact the host or the office named in the announcement to ask whether applications are still being considered. Do not assume an extension or late place is available. If the call is closed, look for another opportunity.

Browse programme listings and host links →

What should I do if I want to withdraw after applying?

Write to the host and inform the relevant IAU office if it is involved. Ask for written confirmation of the withdrawal and check the cancellation, refund, and any grant-agreement terms that apply to you. Check travel and accommodation bookings separately.

Read the guidance on changes after acceptance →
03

Costs & funding

Plan the whole experience.

Where can I see the total programme cost?

Start with the listing’s fee and the host’s current cost breakdown. The advertised fee may not be your full budget: add any travel, accommodation, meals, insurance, visa, and personal expenses that are not covered. Ask the host about anything missing from the breakdown.

See programme fees and host details → See Language Programmes →

What do “included” and “excluded” costs mean?

“Included” means covered by the stated programme fee. “Excluded” means outside that fee and something you may need to budget for separately. What is covered differs between programmes; use the host’s itemised description rather than assuming a package includes everything.

Check what each programme includes →

Are scholarships or discounts available?

Some partner programmes offer support or reduced fees for IAU students. Availability and conditions vary, so check the individual announcement. A partner discount is separate from an Erasmus+ grant; neither should be assumed until confirmed through the relevant process.

Check the programme’s funding information →
04

Credits & travel

Prepare with confidence.

Can I receive academic credit for a programme?

Credit recognition needs to be agreed with your faculty. A certificate or advertised ECTS value alone does not confirm that a partner course will count towards your IAU degree. For Erasmus+ study, agree recognition in the Learning Agreement before mobility and keep the required academic records.

Read the academic approval checklist →

Should I ask my faculty before applying?

Yes, especially if you want academic credit or your plans could affect classes or assessments at IAU. Share the course description and ask your coordinator about approval and the records you will need. Confirm recognition arrangements before committing.

See academic approval and coordinator guidance →

Do I need a passport, visa, or travel insurance?

Check the destination’s official entry rules for your nationality, travel document, and length and purpose of stay, including any transit country. Confirm insurance requirements with the host and destination authorities. Acceptance by a university does not replace permission to enter a country.

Read After You Are Accepted: travel preparation → Official Schengen visa guidance ↗
05

Updates & support

Stay informed and connected.

How often does IAU update the programme lists?

IAU states that it updates the summer/winter list as information arrives from partner universities; it does not give a fixed timetable there. Check the live listing and student announcements, and confirm current details with the host. The saved listings in this folder do not refresh automatically.

Check the live IAU programme list ↗

Who should I contact if I cannot find an answer?

Contact the Short-Term Study Programs Office with the programme name, host university, and your question. Ask the host about its course or booking terms, your faculty about academic recognition, and the IAU Erasmus+ Office about Erasmus+ arrangements.

Find the IAU office team and contact details →
